    About me

    I'm Erik Díaz, I'm 22 and I'm the tattoo artist behind ER·INK.

    I've spent my whole life drawing, since I was a kid, in classes and exhibitions, following in the footsteps of my father, a visual artist who was my biggest motivation. I started tattooing in 2021 with the support of my family, who gave me my first tattoo kit.

    Between tutorials, going to watch other artists tattoo live and asking about every doubt that came up, I found my own way.

    Today I work permanently in Pinar del Río, Cuba, and occasionally in Santiago de Veraguas, Panama. I specialize mostly in black and grey realism, blackwork and neo-traditional.

      Aftercare

      A well-healed tattoo looks good for years. Here's what to do in the first few weeks.

      First hours

      • Keep the film on for as long as I tell you (usually between 3 and 24 hours).
      • Wash your hands, take it off and wash the tattoo with lukewarm water and mild soap.
      • Pat it dry with clean paper towel. Don't rub.

      First 2 weeks

      • Wash it twice a day and apply a thin layer of healing cream.
      • Itching and peeling are normal. Don't scratch or pick at it.
      • Wear clean, loose clothes that don't rub the area.

      Avoid for 3 weeks

      • The beach, pools, rivers and saunas.
      • Direct sun on the tattoo.
      • Shaving or waxing the area.

      For life

      • High sunscreen (SPF 50) whenever you're out in the sun.
      • Caribbean sun fades lines and colour faster than anything else.
      • Moisturised skin keeps a tattoo looking its best.

      If the redness gets worse, the area feels hot, there's pus or you have a fever, see a doctor and message me.

      FAQ

      How much does a tattoo cost?

      It depends on the size, the detail and the placement. The minimum is 40 USD. Send me your idea and I'll give you an exact quote, no strings attached.

      I'm on holiday in Cuba. Can I get tattooed?

      Yes, it's very common. Ideally do it at the end of your trip: for three weeks you won't be able to swim in the sea or sunbathe that area. Message me before you arrive and I'll save you a date.

      Do you design it or can I bring my own?

      Both. You can bring your drawing and I'll adapt it so it works on skin, or we start from your idea and I design it from scratch.

      Does it hurt?

      A bit, and it depends on the placement and on you. Ribs, feet and elbows tend to hurt more; arms and thighs, less. We take breaks whenever you need.

      How old do I need to be?

      18 or older, with photo ID.

      Is it safe?

      I use single-use disposable needles and cartridges, new gloves and barriers at every appointment, and everything is disinfected between clients.

      Do you cover up old tattoos?

      Yes, I do cover-ups and reworks. Send me a well-lit photo of the tattoo you have and I'll tell you what's possible.

      Are touch-ups included?

      Yes. If it needs one, the first touch-up is free within 3 months.
